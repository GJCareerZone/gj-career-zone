# GJ Career Zone — how to use it

Three short guides. Print the first one for students, the second for the
mentor panel, the third for the office.

---

## For students

**Getting in.** Open the platform link, choose *Register as student*, and
fill in your name, roll number, programme, email and a password. You sign in
with the same email afterwards. Forgot the password? Use *Forgot password?*
on the sign-in page — a reset link goes to your email.

**First job: the profiling form.** It asks about your background, marks,
skills, career goals, and the kind of support you want. Fill it once. You
can come back and update it whenever something changes. Until it is
submitted, you cannot raise a counselling request — the office needs your
background before it can act.

**Asking for counselling.** Go to *Ask for counselling*. Tick the areas
where you want help, describe what is going on in your own words, say how
soon you would like to meet and whether you prefer face to face or online.
Raise a request as often as you need. There is no limit and no quota.

**What happens next.** The Career Zone office reads your request, picks a
faculty mentor and fixes a time. You get an alert, and the meeting shows up
on your dashboard. Track the stage of any request on *My requests*:

`Submitted → Assigned → Scheduled → Reviewed → Closed`

**After the meeting.** Your mentor files a short review. Open *Session
records* to see what was agreed, what you are meant to do next, and when the
next review is.

**Who can read what you wrote.** The Career Zone office and the mentor
assigned to you. No other student, and no other faculty member. Discussions
stay confidential except where disclosure is needed for your safety or
someone else's.

**If it is urgent.** Choose *Immediately (within 24–48 hours)* — those
requests go to the top of the office queue. If you are in crisis and cannot
wait, contact the campus counsellor or a helpline directly rather than
waiting for a reply here.

## For faculty mentors

**Your account.** The Career Zone office creates it and gives you a
temporary password. Sign in on the same page students use, then change the
password via *Forgot password?*.

**Assigned to me.** Every student the office gives you appears here with
their request, the areas of concern, the meeting time and any note the
office added. Open *Student profile* before the meeting — the profiling
form tells you their marks, family situation, goals and what they said they
were struggling with.

**Record a session.** After the meeting, open the request and choose
*Record this session*. The mentor–mentee review form opens, pre-filled with
the student's details. If you have met this student before, the action
points from last time are carried over so you can mark what happened to
each.

Fill in what matters and keep it factual: what was reviewed, what the
student said, your assessment, the revised action plan for the next two to
four weeks, and the next review date. Choosing *Close case* as the decision
closes the request; anything else keeps it open for the next review.

**When to escalate.** Answer *Yes* to "needs escalation" if the issue is
beyond mentoring — financial hardship needing institutional help, a
disciplinary matter, or anything touching a student's safety. The office is
alerted immediately.

**Student files.** Everyone you have mentored, with their full request and
review history. Use it before a follow-up so the student does not have to
start their story again.

## For the Career Zone office (admin)

**Overview.** Urgent requests first, then the size of the queue, how many
profiles are on file, how many cases each mentor is carrying, and how many
escalations are open.

**Working the queue.** *Counselling requests* opens on *Needs action*.
Urgent requests sort to the top; the rest sit oldest first. For each one:

- **Assign a mentor** — the dropdown shows each mentor's current open
  caseload, so you can spread the work.
- Set the date, time, mode and room or meeting link. Adding a time moves the
  request to *Scheduled*; leaving it blank leaves it at *Assigned*.
- Add a note for the mentor (background they should know) and a message for
  the student (anything they should bring or prepare).
- **Assign and notify** alerts both of them.

**Oversight.** *Profiling forms* holds form 1 for every student, *Mentor
reviews* holds form 3 for every session, and *Open full file* on any student
shows their profile, every request and every review in one place.

**Faculty.** Add mentors, deactivate anyone who leaves, or promote a
colleague to admin. Deactivating keeps all their history; removing a record
does not delete their sign-in, which has to be done in the Firebase console.

**Activity log.** Who submitted, assigned, exported or deleted what, and
when. It cannot be edited, including by you.

**Exports.** Every tab has *Export CSV*. Run them weekly — it is your backup
and it is also the data behind any NAAC or AICTE report you have to write.

**Two habits worth keeping.** Assign urgent requests the same working day,
and close cases when they are genuinely finished — an accurate queue is the
only way the dashboard stays useful.
