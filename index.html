How the desk works

Four records, one thread per student

  1. Register and profileFill the student profiling form once. It reaches the Career Zone office and stays with your file.
  2. Raise a requestWhenever you need to talk, submit a counselling request. Raise as many as you need.
  3. Get a mentor and a slotThe office assigns a faculty mentor, fixes the date and notifies you both.
  4. Session on recordYour mentor files a review form after the meeting, so the next one starts where this one ended.
